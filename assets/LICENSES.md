# Asset provenance

- Car Concept vehicle: Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Based on an original public-domain concept by Unity Fan. Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept. Adaptations: removed sample branding, changed paint and Godot material response, corrected AO UV selection, added wheel animation and modular upgrades. Full license and attribution are in `vehicles/concept/`. Attribution is also accessible in the game's Credits screen. No assets were extracted from Zombie Highway or Earn to Die.
- Garage, terrain, modular parts and zombie stand-ins: original procedural geometry in the project scripts. Surface textures and tree cutouts are original generated assets; their generator is included in `tools/generate_surfaces.py`.
- Icon and interface design: original project artwork.
- Engine sound: original synthesized waveform generated at runtime.
- Barlow Regular and Barlow Condensed Bold: Jeremy Tribby / Barlow contributors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Font files from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlow and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed. License text included in `fonts/OFL.txt` (the same license covers both families).
- Godot engine: MIT license, https://godotengine.org/license/. Android templates include their own third-party notices. Include the engine/third-party acknowledgments in a distribution's credits before release.

The project has no chosen overall source-code license yet. Asset provenance does not grant a license to the entire game.
